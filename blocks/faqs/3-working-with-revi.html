Working with REVI

Who is monthly bookkeeping built for?

Flippers doing 2 or more flips a year, and landlords with 2 or more rentals. At that point there are enough transactions each month that the books stop being a once-a-year cleanup and start being something worth keeping current. With a single property, a spreadsheet and a good CPA at year end usually covers it.

When is the right time to bring in a bookkeeper?

Usually around the second or third property, or the second flip running at the same time. One property you can track by hand. What changes is the number of transactions: more accounts, more contractor payments, draws, closing statements, and a year-end pile that stops being something you can reconstruct from memory in April.

Why hand off bookkeeping for rental properties?

Every rental adds work each month. Rent, repairs, and loan payments all need to be recorded and reconciled, with principal kept apart from interest, so you can see what each property actually makes. Kept current, the books give you NOI by property all year and a year-end package your CPA can file from.

What's the difference between a bookkeeper and a CPA?

Different jobs, and most investors need both. Bookkeeping is the month-to-month work, recording transactions, reconciling accounts, tracking costs by property, and producing the reports. A CPA takes those reports at year end, applies tax law, and files the return. The cleaner the books are, the less there is for the CPA to reconstruct.

Do you work directly with my CPA?

Yes. At year end, we put together a clean package for your CPA: Schedule E prep and depreciation schedules for rentals, per-flip cost breakdowns for sold flips, and the documentation behind the numbers.

Do you file taxes?

No. REVI is bookkeeping. The books, the monthly reports, and the year-end package come from us. The return is filed by your CPA.

Cleanup, if there's any, is quoted separately as a one-time project.

QuickBooks Online. The setup is built around your flips and properties, which is what makes per-flip and property-level reporting possible. If you're on spreadsheets or another platform, moving over is part of the setup.
